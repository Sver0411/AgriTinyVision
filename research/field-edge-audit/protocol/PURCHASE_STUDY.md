# Buying target labels versus reviewing frames

Lock for the prospective computation, after the earlier cohorts and earlier head performance had been seen. This is a new comparison on previously studied sites, without public preregistration. Do not tune the controller using held-out performance. Keep the PDF unchanged for this results review.

* Use common disjoint target calibration/test identities in Cornell, Kashmir and Latvia. Keep 20 nested, content-hash draws of 16, 32 and 128 reference candidates and 5/5/1 evaluation arrival orders. Latvia uses earlier/later calendar days. The camera may store a separate reservoir of calibration candidates or their addresses and JPEG lengths before the deployment window. Future evaluation frames, scores, lengths, labels and remote decisions are excluded from setup. Use all three pipelines: released FP32 V2, released-scale host V2 grid and FP32 V4, with native source operating points. The single cloud expert is unchanged.
* A scheduled window with N arrivals has 6800 nominal application bytes per frame at full rate, fixed for every site, method and seed. Compare 10/25/50% contracts. Prepaid supplies this quota upfront. Earned supplies exactly 680/1700/3400 bytes at each arrival from zero balance with no borrowing. Both debit supervision, deployment and serving. The main comparison is 25%, both 32/128 caps and both funding modes. Existing calibration JPEGs differ in encoded length. A human label costs its JPEG plus 8 bytes of image envelope, a D+13 byte activation and identifier record, a 4-byte source score and a 12-byte receipt; the service observes its own prediction on that already-paid JPEG. Each purchase episode includes a 32-byte request quoting the window, contract and candidate exchange prices, plus an 8-byte controller action packet. The shared packed classifier is D+14 bytes. Dedicated learned routers add their actual serialized parameters. The published fixed gate also has its own threshold/temperature bytes. Each inference query costs the actual JPEG plus 20 bytes. This experiment counts application bytes and assumes immediate service upon payment. No physical energy, expert turnaround or farm RTT is claimed.
* The proposed controller rejects a pilot before labeling when the complete 16-image exchange, including its fitted head and action packet, exceeds half the total allowance. This deterministic economic screen reserves at least as much bandwidth as the initial probe costs. On acceptance, buy exactly the first 16 target labels, and deliver their fitted shadow head even if subsequently discarded. Assess each candidate using four deterministic ID-position folds on those 16, comparing nested 6- and 12-training-record heads on the same four held-out pilot cases per fold. Estimate review opportunities using pilot-only uncertainty, label-independent pilot order, quoted fees, planned horizon and evaluation-blind expected JPEG price. Under earned funding, include an initial source-only period in that pilot forecast. The empirical pilot forecast is an estimate, not held-out test performance or an error guarantee. Expansion buys the remaining labels in the authorization and delivers a second head. All pilot and shadow overheads remain spent if the update is discarded. Read the companion selector protocol for the paired shrinkage and sensitivity condition.
* Actual streaming uses only the current local scores and JPEG cost, the past 64 scores, a running size mean, and the known quota/horizon. Following a 16-frame burn-in after deployment, send if the urgency is in the funded upper fraction of the previous scores. The calibrated EdgeBoost fixed threshold uses its original score criterion and the same hard budget, with no burn-in. Equal urgency receives its midrank. When acquiring a panel under earned funding, keep serving original local classes and reserve until the setup is fully funded. Pilot choices may be precalculated for replay only because neither their scores nor their purchases may affect service before the corresponding debit becomes affordable. During runs, total outflow must fit every accrued prefix. Test labels are opened by the scorer only after generating decisions. Cloud predictions on the evaluation stream affect only sent photographs.
* The protected variant preserves every positive alert of the original native camera before and after review, and avoids querying those images. This gives a pointwise FN ceiling versus the original source camera and can make eliminating existing false alerts impossible. Report false positives, false negatives, BA, ordinary accuracy and costs alongside this structural guarantee. Give fixed policies and matched published routers the same option.
* Compare no purchase, fixed maximum purchase (EdgeBoost rank/fixed, OvA, CompareConfidence, Mao two-stage, Diff-01, Gatekeeper and continued CE), BBSD-soft-triggered maximum purchase, a 16-label fixed probe, and paired empirical selection on the same pilot. For exact annotation matching, give published algorithms precisely the target labels the controller actually bought, including the paid pilot when it chose to discard its head. EdgeBoost rank with the pilot/final receipts shares the exact setup cost and release time; learned routers pay the difference in actual wire size or fall back if unaffordable. These retrospectively matched controls inherit the proposed purchase decision and do not themselves estimate when to purchase. Static policies with fewer actual labels retain their unspent budget. Never silently interpret shared authorizations as equal realized spending.
* Report per-condition paired effects across all three sites and all three feature pipelines, including ties, reversals and fallback frequency. Seeds and arrival reorderings share images. Resample paired images within class, or entire Latvian capture days, holding trained policies and their actual routing decisions fixed. These describe the finite cohorts. A successful across-site algorithm claim requires positive effects against both fixed and label-matched strong comparators and no evidence of a Latvia recall/false-alarm collapse. The no-borrowing setting and the V4 and V2-grid results are primary evidence. Report any non-wins.
