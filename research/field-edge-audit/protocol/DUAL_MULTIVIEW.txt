Follow-up on simultaneous improvements after the original operating-point comparisons. The earlier Cornell and Kashmir aggregate error profiles have already been viewed. The following is an exploratory additional acquisition strategy. Its classifier, fitting sample and threshold rule are fixed before computing the multiview predictions.
One 448-long-edge Q75 JPEG is transmitted per test frame. At the receiving end take a squashed full scene and two square end crops along the long axis, each resized to 224 square, extracting frozen DINOv2-small CLS tokens. Use a normalized concatenation of the full token and the mean and elementwise max of the two local tokens. The classifier is L2 logistic regression C=1 on standardized features, with balanced class weights. Fix deterministic split of the existing 450 Cornell representatives: SHA256('multi-v1/'+path) ordering, first 300 for fitting, remaining 150 for calibrating one operating threshold. Enumerate calibration score cutoffs and compare against the published edge model's FP/FN on the same calibration panel. Choose the cutoff maximizing the smaller relative reduction (denominators floored at one); break ties by larger summed gain and then lower threshold. Fall back to original edge if neither error improves in this calibration panel. Do not select thresholds on any evaluation set. Evaluate this one frozen policy on the exact same Cornell1353 and Kashmir384. Treat cross-source failure and the full training and transmission costs as results. Transparent secondary positive counts from previously computed sweep do not constitute selection of a deployable policy.
