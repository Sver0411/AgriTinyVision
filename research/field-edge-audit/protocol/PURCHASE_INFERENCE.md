# Paired analysis of the purchasing experiment

The primary plots use the nominal 25% service budget, a 32-label ceiling, both up-front and arriving byte credits, and the same-label `matched_rank` control. Each policy still pays for all calibration and deployment at cold start. The full paired table includes the 128-label ceiling and the predeclared frozen, always-EdgeBoost, always-OvA and matched guarded comparisons. No choice of seed, operating point or site enters this definition. The two label ceilings give permission to buy up to that many labels. Only expenditure-matched controls can support claims about use of an identical number of purchased labels. The second protected policy is compared to guarded baselines and to the unprotected adaptive policy to quantify its false-positive cost. Computational V2 grid results approximate one numerical implementation of the V2 architecture. They are not a third independent model architecture or new physical ESP32-S3 measurements.

After every online choice and label purchase is frozen, the evaluation script aligns the packed predictions to privately held reference labels by opaque test identity. It checks FP, FN and BA against every primary-budget run before averaging. Each within-policy mean gives equal weight to matched calibration draws and stream orders. Each per-image paired contrast counts the difference in calls, averaged over these matched replays. Positive changes in FNR and FPR mean additional errors. Delta BA equals minus one half the sum of the FPR and FNR differences in percentage points. False-positive and false-negative differences in the table are expected counts per evaluated cohort across draws, so they may be fractional.

Paired percentile intervals use 2,000 coupled resamples. Cornell and Kashmir are resampled by test photo within true class. The processed Kashmir source lacks parent-photograph identifiers; fitted intervals can be narrower than intervals based on independent plants. For Latvia, the nine complete held-out dates are sampled as whole clusters, preserving every image of each sampled date and conditioning on bootstrap samples that contain both classes. We report the number of usable resamples. The distribution is discrete at nine dates. Heads, calibration panels, realized query masks, funding schedules and seeded arrival orders remain fixed in these intervals. These intervals do not estimate generalization to new orchards, quantify re-training/queueing uncertainty following a changed sample, or provide simultaneous familywise coverage. Seed win counts are descriptive since the same photographs are reused. Combined files may be checked with `python scripts/purchase_inference.py --out results/purchase`. The script also accepts nine `runs_SITE_BACKBONE.csv` shards when the combined file is absent.

The supplementary paired comparisons expose each other label-matched published method, the published-monitor acquisition wrapper, the empirical BA selector, and the guarded policy's own label-matched controls. Rows explicitly count equal realized label use and equal setup bytes. Panel identities are nested and shared, so an identical number bought in a matched seed means the same purchased calibration identities. Different-sized downloaded routers may still cost different numbers of bytes within the common total allowance.
