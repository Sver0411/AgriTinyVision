# Additional diagnostic details

Guard-matched controls use the number of labels paid by their respective adaptive controller. The supplementary operating point and targeting tables include both pairs.

A union guard preserves every original positive prediction. Its source false positives cannot be removed. Consequently, even an oracle achieving sensitivity one has BA at most (1 + source specificity)/2. For each backbone we report this upper bound, the remaining possible absolute BA gain from resolving all source misses, and the source-derived FP/FN limits. We test the pointwise inclusion property against every saved guarded decision.

For interpretation after inspecting these results, the machine-readable primary table also breaks out the zero-label protected frozen policy versus the native camera and versus unprotected review. These contrasts can be recovered directly from the prespecified operating points.
